Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About FASTIDOX

What is intermittent fasting and how does it promote wellness?
Intermittent fasting is an eating pattern structured around time windows rather than strict food restrictions. By giving your body extended periods between meals, circulating insulin drops, signaling cells to release stored body fat for energy while activating autophagy and cellular housekeeping pathways.
Do I need to register an account or share my email?
No. FASTIDOX requires no registration, no passwords, and no email address. Simply install the app and begin tracking immediately with complete anonymity.
Is intermittent fasting safe for everyone?
Intermittent fasting is not suitable for everyone. It is strongly advised against for pregnant or nursing mothers, individuals under 18, anyone with a personal history of eating disorders, or individuals with type 1 diabetes or medications that require meal synchronization. FASTIDOX includes an automated self-screening tool and is not a replacement for certified medical counsel.
How does FASTIDOX calculate estimated fat burn?
The application calculates your personal Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the validated Mifflin-St Jeor formula, tailored to your biological sex, age, height, and weight. It then models a physiological fat-oxidation curve based on hours fasted.
Can I back up my fasting records if I change phones?
Yes. FASTIDOX supports encrypted operating system backup (Android Auto Backup / Google Drive) tied directly to your private Google account, as well as a full manual JSON export and import feature in the Settings screen.

Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare provider before undertaking significant dietary changes or extended fasting protocols.
