Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Summary: FASTIDOX ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. The application is built on a local-first, privacy-by-design architecture. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not operate a remote server or database storing your personal health data.

Your health, fasting, and physical wellness records stay stored securely on your own device. The third-party service providers we use (Google AdMob for advertisements, RevenueCat for subscription management, Sentry for anonymous crash diagnostics, and Android Auto Backup) operate strictly under the transparent disclosures detailed below.

1. Scope & Controller Information

This Privacy Policy applies to the FASTIDOX mobile application available on the Google Play Store (and other authorized mobile app marketplaces). If you have any inquiries, requests, or privacy concerns, you can reach our team at: orenliran@gmail.com.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Because FASTIDOX is an intermittent fasting, hydration, and wellness tracking tool, the application processes information that you voluntarily enter into the interface. All personal and health-related data listed below is stored solely within the local sandboxed storage on your device (AsyncStorage) and is never transmitted to our servers:

A. Profile & Physical Metrics

  • Display name or nickname (optional, used only to personalize in-app interface greetings)
  • Biological sex, age, height, current body weight, and target weight
  • Body fat percentage and self-selected body build / muscularity (if provided)
  • Personal wellness objectives, daily physical activity levels, and prior fasting experience

B. Health & Safety Screening Data

To assist in user safety and provide automated safeguards against medically inappropriate fasting protocols, the app presents an initial self-screening questionnaire that asks whether any of the following apply:

  • Pregnancy, planning pregnancy, or breastfeeding
  • History or current diagnosis of eating disorders (e.g., anorexia, bulimia, binge eating)
  • Type 1 diabetes or diabetes managed with medication/insulin
  • Prescription medications that must be consumed with meals or on a strict schedule
  • Other chronic illnesses or metabolic conditions

Strict Privacy Guarantee: Your responses to the health screening questionnaire are processed exclusively by on-device algorithms to display appropriate warnings and safety guidelines. They are never transmitted to any external server, never shared with third parties, and never used for marketing or advertising purposes.

C. Fasting & Activity Logs

  • Fasting records: start and end timestamps, planned vs. actual duration, and metabolic stage milestones
  • Hydration logs: daily water intake amounts and reminders
  • Nutrition logs: meal timestamps and meal quality ratings
  • Workouts, activity sessions, and metabolic rest days
  • Weight log entries and historical progress over time

D. App Preferences & Settings

  • Selected fasting schedule (e.g., 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, or custom) and daily start hour
  • Local notification preferences and quiet hours
  • User interface language preference
  • Timestamp confirming your acceptance of the Medical Disclaimer

3. Third-Party Services & SDK Disclosures

To support the free tier with advertising, process optional in-app subscriptions, and ensure app stability across various Android devices, FASTIDOX integrates certified third-party software development kits (SDKs). These services process technical, diagnostic, or transactional data as described below:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

The free version of FASTIDOX displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC):

  • Data Collected: Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS), device identifiers, IP address (used for approximate coarse location at the city/country level and ad fraud detection), device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics.
  • Non-Personalized Ads & Privacy: The app is configured to request non-personalized ads (NPA) in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and data safety standards.
  • Complete Isolation: FASTIDOX never shares, links, transfers, or correlates your personal health data, weight, age, fasting metrics, or health screening responses with Google AdMob or any third-party advertisers.
  • For further details, consult Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

B. RevenueCat & Google Play Billing (Subscriptions)

If you choose to purchase an optional premium subscription or in-app upgrade, the transaction is processed securely through Google Play Billing, with entitlement management handled by RevenueCat:

  • Data Processed: RevenueCat receives an anonymous App User ID, transaction receipt/token, and entitlement status to unlock premium features and facilitate subscription restoration across your devices.
  • No Payment Card Data Held: FASTIDOX never receives, stores, or accesses your credit card, debit card, or financial billing credentials. All payment processing is conducted securely by Google Play.
  • For more information, visit RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

C. Sentry (Technical Crash & Error Reporting)

We use Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) to detect, diagnose, and resolve technical bugs and application crashes:

  • Data Collected: Technical stack traces, error codes, device hardware specifications, operating system version, app build number, and timestamp of the incident.
  • Strict Data Sanitization: Crash telemetry is filtered before transmission to guarantee that no personal profile data, fasting logs, or health screening answers are ever included. Reports are not linked to identifiable user accounts.
  • Learn more at Sentry's Privacy Policy.

D. Android Auto Backup (Google Drive)

FASTIDOX supports Android's standard Auto Backup feature. If enabled on your device, the Android operating system periodically uploads an encrypted snapshot of the app's local sandbox data to your personal Google Drive storage. This ensures that your fasting history is retained when you migrate to a new device.

  • This backup is transmitted directly between your device and Google's infrastructure. We have no access to your Google Drive account or your backup archives.
  • You can disable this feature at any time through your device settings under Settings → Google → Backup.

4. Local Notifications

FASTIDOX schedules reminders (such as fasting start/end notifications and hydration prompts) exclusively using your device's local notification scheduler (via Notifee). No external push notification tokens, remote dispatch servers, or third-party message brokers are involved. You may configure or disable notifications at any time in the app's settings or via system settings.

5. Practices We Do NOT Engage In

  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or health data.
  • We do not require user accounts, email sign-ups, or social logins.
  • We do not access your precise GPS location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, photo library, microphone, or external storage files.
  • We do not provide health data to data brokers, ad networks, or third-party marketers.

6. Data Retention, Portability & Instant Deletion

In full alignment with the Google Play Data Deletion Policy and global privacy standards, you maintain absolute control over all information stored by the app:

  • Instant Local Deletion: You can permanently wipe all logs, profile information, and health screening records directly within the app at any time via: Settings → Backup & restore → Delete all my data.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling FASTIDOX immediately and permanently removes all local sandbox files and cached data from your device.
  • Data Export: You can generate and download a complete JSON export of your stored records at any time via: Settings → Backup & restore → Export Backup.
  • No Server-Side Retention: Because we do not store your data on external servers, there is no server-side retention period and no residual data stored by us once deleted locally.

7. Children's Privacy (18+ Policy)

FASTIDOX is strictly intended for individuals who are at least 18 years of age. Intermittent fasting can cause physiological imbalances and growth complications in minors. We do not knowingly market to, collect, or process information from individuals under 18 years of age. If we learn that an individual under 18 has submitted data, we will immediately take steps to ensure that data is deleted.

8. International Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Global Standards)

Regardless of your geographic location, FASTIDOX respects your international privacy rights:

  • European Economic Area (EEA) & UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and export your personal data. Because all information is stored locally on your device, you exercise these rights directly through the app (editing profile, exporting backup, deleting all data).
  • California Residents (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell or share your personal information. You have the right to know what data is collected and to request its complete deletion.
  • Global Users: Our privacy-by-design, local-first model adheres to fair information practices and data minimization principles worldwide.

The updated date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest revision. Material changes affecting data handling will also be highlighted in app release notes.
