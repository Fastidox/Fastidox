Delete Your Data

FASTIDOX by MATRIF · Last updated: October 31, 2026

FASTIDOX has no user accounts and keeps your profile, fasting logs, weight, meals and health answers only on your phone. We do not hold a copy on any server, so you can delete all of it yourself, immediately, without asking us.

1. Delete everything from inside the app

  1. Open FASTIDOX and go to the Me tab (Profile & Settings).
  2. Tap Backup & restore.
  3. Tap Delete all my data and confirm twice.

This erases every fast, weight, meal, health answer and setting from the device, and from the app's Android Auto Backup in your Google account. It cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you might want any of it back.

2. Uninstalling

Uninstalling FASTIDOX removes its data from the phone. If Android Auto Backup is on, Google may keep the app's last backup in your Google Drive and restore it on reinstall; delete it in Google Drive → Backups, or use Delete all my data before uninstalling.

3. Data held by our service providers

  • Crash reports (Sentry): technical reports with no health data, not linked to you. They are deleted automatically when Sentry's retention period ends (at most 90 days).
  • Subscriptions (Google Play, RevenueCat): an anonymous purchase ID and the purchase record. Google Play keeps its own order history under its policies; to have the RevenueCat record deleted, email us (below).
  • Ads (Google AdMob): you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

4. Ask us

For any deletion request or question, email matrif.studio@gmail.com with the subject "FASTIDOX data deletion request". We reply within 30 days. See also our Privacy Policy.
